How this diary answers

Write on the page, then rest your pen. The page drinks your ink and answers in its own hand.

For parents

This is the children's copy. It follows fixed rules for young writers, keeps this cover behind your PIN, and keeps a record of every page. Tell your children that you can read what they write here.

The PIN opens this cover. If it is lost, clear this site's data in the browser's settings: the diary then starts afresh, without its key, memory or record.

The children's rules

These go out with every page and come before the character you set below. They cannot be changed here.
